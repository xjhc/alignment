# Community: Moderation & Player Safety Guide

## 1. Our Philosophy: Play Hard, Be Kind

`Alignment` is a game of deception, paranoia, and high-stakes accusations. Passionate debate is expected and encouraged. However, there is a clear line between strategic, in-game conflict and real-world harassment. Our goal is to foster a competitive but respectful community where players feel safe to engage.

This guide outlines our rules of conduct and the system for enforcing them. It is designed to be simple, fair, and transparent. By playing `Alignment`, you agree to these rules.

## 2. The Rules of Conduct

To ensure a positive experience for everyone, all players must adhere to the following rules. Failure to do so will result in moderation action.

1.  **Be Respectful.** Do not use language that is hateful, abusive, sexually explicit, defamatory, or discriminatory. This includes attacks based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, religion, or disability.
2.  **No Harassment or Bullying.** Do not engage in targeted, repetitive, or severe harassment of other players. This includes stalking, threats of violence, or encouraging others to harass a player. In-game accusations are part of the game; real-world personal attacks are not.
3.  **Keep it In-Game.** The deception and accusations of `Alignment` are for the game only. Do not take in-game rivalries into DMs, other platforms, or real life.
4.  **No Cheating or Exploiting.** Do not use third-party software, bots, scripts, or game exploits to gain an unfair advantage. This includes "stream sniping" or using out-of-game communication channels to coordinate with other players in the same game (unless you are in the `#aligned` channel).
5.  **Protect Your and Others' Privacy.** Do not share personal information about yourself or other players without their explicit consent. This includes real names, addresses, or other private data.

## 3. The Reporting & Moderation Process

We use a simple, community-assisted moderation system.

#### **Step 1: In-Game Reporting**

*   **How it Works:** If you encounter a player violating the rules, you can report a specific chat message. The "Report Message" action is available on the message interaction toolbar.
*   **What it Does:** When you submit a report, you will be asked to select a category (e.g., "Harassment," "Hate Speech," "Cheating"). The report, along with the relevant chat context, is sent to our moderation queue. **It does not automatically take action.**

#### **Step 2: Review & Action**

*   **Who Reviews:** Reports are reviewed by our community moderation team.
*   **The Process:** Moderators will review the reported message and surrounding chat logs to determine if a violation occurred. They use a standardized enforcement matrix to ensure actions are consistent.

#### **Step 3: Enforcement Actions**

Our moderation actions are tiered. Repeat offenses will result in more severe penalties.

*   **Warning:** A formal, recorded warning sent to the player.
*   **Chat Mute (Temporary):** The player is muted from text chat for a period of 1 to 7 days.
*   **Game Suspension (Temporary):** The player is unable to join any games for a period of 3 to 30 days.
*   **Permanent Ban:** The player's account is permanently banned from playing `Alignment`. This is reserved for severe or repeat offenses.

#### **Step 4: Appeals**

*   Players who receive a suspension or ban will be notified with the reason and a link to our support system where they can appeal the decision. All appeals are reviewed by a senior moderator.

We believe this system strikes the right balance between empowering players to report bad behavior and ensuring a fair, human-reviewed process for enforcement. Our goal is to educate and correct behavior first, and only resort to bans for those who cannot or will not participate constructively in the community.
