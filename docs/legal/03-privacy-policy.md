# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** [Date]

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Loebian Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our game, Alignment (the "Service").

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

*   **Information You Provide to Us:**
    *   **Account Information:** When you create an account, we collect your chosen username (handle) and a way to authenticate you (e.g., email and a hashed password, or an ID from a third-party provider like Discord or Google).
    *   **Communications:** We collect the content you submit in-game, such as chat messages and player reports.

*   **Information We Collect Automatically:**
    *   **Gameplay Data:** We collect data about your in-game actions, such as votes cast, achievements earned, and game outcomes, to provide meta-progression features and perform game balance analysis.
    *   **Log and Usage Data:** We log technical information when you use our Service, which may include your IP address, browser type, and timestamps. This is used for security and to debug our Service.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

*   Provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
*   Create and manage your account.
*   Enforce our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
*   Communicate with you about your account or important service updates.
*   Protect the security and integrity of our Service and our players.
*   Analyze game balance and player engagement to make the game better (this is done with aggregated, anonymized data).

**We will never sell your personal information.**

### 3. How We Store and Protect Your Information

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access.

*   **Data Security:** We use technical measures like encryption and access controls. Passwords, if used, are always hashed.
*   **Data Retention:** We believe in data minimization.
    *   **Game Data:** All chat logs and detailed event logs for a specific game are automatically and permanently deleted from our servers **7 days** after the game concludes.
    *   **Operational Logs:** Server logs containing IP addresses are kept for a maximum of **30 days**.
    *   **Account Data:** Your account information and meta-progression stats are kept as long as your account is active.

### 4. Your Choices & Rights

You have rights and choices regarding your information:

*   **Account Information:** You can review and update your username and other profile information in your account settings.
*   **Account Deletion:** You can request to permanently delete your account at any time through your profile settings. When you delete your account, we will delete your personal information, such as your email and authentication ID. Your gameplay stats may be anonymized and retained in aggregate for statistical purposes.
*   **Data Portability:** Depending on your location, you may have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the Service or by other means.
